Lumen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Lumen (also published as Reading Partner) is a reading app. This page describes what the app sends to our own server when you sign in with Google, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only after you have agreed to this policy in the app, and only while you are signed in with Google, the app sends our server:

  • your Google account ID, email address and name, taken from the Google sign-in;
  • a random device ID the app generates when it is installed;
  • the device's platform, operating system version and model;
  • the app version.

This is sent when you sign in, and then at most once a day when the app starts. We do not store your IP address. Your books, notes and conversations are not sent to our server: synced data stays in your own Google Drive.

Why

  • To identify your account and the devices it is used on.
  • To support syncing across your devices.
  • To understand how the product is used, through anonymous aggregate analysis (for example, how many devices run each app version).

Sharing

We do not currently provide this data to any third party. The server runs on Vercel and the database on Neon, which store it on our behalf. If we ever want to provide it to a third party, we will ask for your separate consent first.

Deleting your data

To have your account and device records deleted, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address of the Google account you signed in with. Signing out of Google in the app stops any further reports.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
