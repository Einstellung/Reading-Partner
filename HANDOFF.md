## Design decision to record in docs/89

The frontend primes cues (`prime_speech_cues`) before `start_conversation`, so a `Synthesize` can arrive before `Open`. `Synthesize` therefore carries the Kokoro model path and the engine loads Kokoro on demand (`kokoro_at`); `Open` does not load it twice.
